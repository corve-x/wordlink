    How to play

    Every chain has six words. Each word joins the one before it to make a familiar phrase or compound word.

    BLACK → ICE → CREAM

    Black ice, ice cream. You get the first letter and the length; type the rest and the word locks in.

    The clock starts with your first letter. Hints unlock after 15 seconds for a letter and 60 for the whole word.
